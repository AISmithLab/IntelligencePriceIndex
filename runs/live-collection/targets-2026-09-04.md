## What this list cannot fix

Survivorship (step 63B) is upstream of every choice here: the 32.1% that remain listed over-represent expensive, heavily-reviewed gigs, and no ordering of the survivors recovers the dead. Any 2026 estimate built from this collection carries that caveat and should be reported with it.
